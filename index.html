Academic Background & Service

Biography

Ali Mohammad Alqudah, Ph.D., in Biomedical Engineering, University of Manitoba, Canada. His research focuses on advancing healthcare solutions through artificial intelligence, particularly in biomedical signal processing and clinical applications. His current work involves developing novel methods for detecting Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) during wakefulness using tracheal breathing sounds, leveraging machine learning and deep learning techniques at the Biomedical Instrumentation and Signal Analysis Lab.

He holds a B.S.E. in Biomedical Systems Engineering (2015) and an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering (Industrial Automation Engineering, 2018) from Yarmouk University, Irbid, Jordan. Prior to joining the University of Manitoba, he served as a Lab Engineer, Teaching & Research Assistant, and Lecturer in the Department of Biomedical Systems and Informatics Engineering at Yarmouk University from 2015 to 2022. He previously served as Chair of the IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society (EMBS), Yarmouk University Student Branch, IEEE Jordan Section.

Committed to driving innovation at the intersection of engineering and medicine, his work aims to develop advanced, AI-driven technologies to improve diagnostic accuracy and patient outcomes.
